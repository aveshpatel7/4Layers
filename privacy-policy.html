Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 30, 2026

At 4Layers Smart Home ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the 4Layers Smart Home mobile app, Cloud API, and integrations with Google Home and Amazon Alexa.

01 Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Account Information: Such as your email address, username, and account ID.
  • Smart Home Device Information: Device names, status (ON/OFF), fan speeds, room assignments, and hardware device identifiers.
  • Diagnostic & Network Data: App usage, diagnostic logs, IP address, and connectivity information.
  • Voice Integration Commands: Smart home commands received through Google Home or Amazon Alexa to perform requested actions. We do not store voice recordings.

02 How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Authenticate your account and maintain overall system security.
  • Control and synchronize your smart home devices seamlessly across mobile apps and voice platforms.
  • Process voice commands received via Google Assistant and Amazon Alexa.
  • Improve app performance, cloud security, and IoT server infrastructure reliability.
  • Provide prompt technical and customer support.

03 Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only with trusted service providers (such as AWS cloud infrastructure) necessary to operate our services or when required by law to protect legal rights.

04 Data Security & Encryption

We protect your information using industry-standard security measures, including TLS/SSL encryption for all API endpoints, secure AWS cloud infrastructure, and access controls. While we work to protect your data, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

05 Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to provide our smart home services, comply with legal obligations, and maintain account functionality. You may request account deletion and removal of associated device data at any time by contacting our support team.

06 Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

07 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
